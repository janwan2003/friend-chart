# friend-chart

## Thesis

**Who:** Friend group chats, flatmates, cousins, sports teams, office teams and classes. These are
teens to forty-somethings who already make "alignment chart" memes of their friend group by hand
(a 3×3 grid from "lawful good" to "chaotic evil", or two silly axes like "chaotic ↔ calm" ×
"always early ↔ always late").

**What:** A no-login group chart where the friends place each other. One person picks a chart
(a 3×3 alignment grid or two axes: "Grinch ↔ Elf" × "plans everything ↔ wings it" for Christmas)
and sends the link to the chat. Each member opens it, types a nickname, drags a dot for
themselves ("where I think I am") and then drags every other member's dot. Positions stay hidden
until everyone has placed everyone (or a deadline passes). Then the chart reveals: each person's
dot sits at the group's average placement, with a faint ring where they put themselves. Awards
come from the data: "Most misjudged" (biggest self vs group gap), "Group consensus" (everyone put
Tom in the same corner), "The wildcard" (the person nobody agrees on). A new chart each week from
a library of themes ("zombie apocalypse survival × usefulness", "texts back fast ↔ never",
"Christmas: Grinch ↔ Elf").

**Needs others:** yes. Your dot is placed by your friends; with one person there is no chart.

**Viral moment:** the revealed chart with everyone's faces or initials on it, plus the
"self vs friends" gap ("I put myself at Lawful Good. My friends put me at Chaotic Evil."). That
gap is the screenshot people post to stories and argue about in the chat.

**How often:** weekly. A new themed chart every week; Christmas and New Year themes in the next
6 weeks; any group can run one in 30 seconds.

**Why now:** the meme exists and is made by hand: TierMaker's "create-xy" alignment templates
for friend groups and inside jokes, ClickUp's "Friend Group Chart" example, Kapwing meme templates.
Every tool we found is a solo editor: one person decides where everyone goes. Nobody turns it into
a group vote with a self-vs-group reveal.

### The six questions

1. **Why the group IS the input:** every dot's final position is the average of the other members'
   placements. One person alone can only place themselves, which is exactly the part that is not
   interesting.
2. **Reveal moment:** when the last member submits (or the deadline hits), all dots slide into
   place at once, then the self-placement rings appear. Nobody sees others' placements before
   submitting theirs.
3. **Ritual or deadline:** weekly chart drop (default Sunday evening), with a reveal deadline
   ("chart reveals at 9 pm or when everyone is in"). Seasonal packs: Christmas, New Year, summer
   holiday.
4. **30-second join:** open link → nickname → drag yourself → drag 5–8 friends → submit. Taps and
   drags only, no typing beyond the nickname.
5. **Screenshot-worthy result:** a square chart card with everyone's initials or emoji avatars,
   the axis labels, and one award line ("Most misjudged: Ana"), sized for stories and WhatsApp.
   The link preview shows live state ("Christmas chart · 5 of 7 have placed everyone").
6. **Why a joiner creates their own:** everyone belongs to several groups (family, work, uni),
   and the reveal ends with "Chart your other group" plus three ready themes. Being placed by
   friends is personal, so people want to see how *another* group sees them.

## Market evidence

Searches run 2026-11-21. Sources are search-result summaries, not opened pages, unless noted.

- **The meme is made by hand today, with solo tools:**
  - TierMaker's XY/alignment templates include friend-group and inside-joke charts, e.g.
    [Someone Familiar Alignment Chart](https://tiermaker.com/create-xy/someone-familiar-17892188),
    [inside jokes chart](https://tiermaker.com/create-xy/poo-poo-stink-memes-18092435),
    ["Quien es el mejor amigo de X"](https://tiermaker.com/create-xy/quien-es-el-mejor-amigo-de-x-nombre-generico-edition-18510078),
    [Member Tierlist](https://tiermaker.com/create-xy/member-tierlist--18541905). One person drags
    the items; a "live voting poll" option is mentioned on some pages but votes on a whole list, not
    placements.
  - TierMaker as a whole is large: Semrush modeled ~17.3M visits in Sept 2026 and ~18.4M in Aug 2026
    (third-party blog citing Semrush, [aiidelist](https://aiidelist.com/blog/tiermaker-founder-viral-growth-case-study);
    unverified, modeled figures).
  - Solo chart makers and templates: [Common Ninja alignment chart maker](https://www.commoninja.com/free-tools/engagement/alignment-chart-maker),
    [ClickUp templates incl. a "Friend Group Chart"](https://clickup.com/blog/alignment-chart-templates/),
    [Miro](https://miro.com/templates/alignment-chart), [Figma/FigJam](https://www.figma.com/templates/alignment-chart-template/),
    [Lucid](https://lucid.co/templates/alignment-chart).
  - Meme history: alignment charts became a meme once people assigned real people and friends to
    them ([Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/unclick/alignment-chart-meme/)); a Kapwing template is
    pitched "for sorting your friend group" ([Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/explore/children-yelling-mcdonalds-mcdonalds-mcdonalds-alignment-chart-meme-template)).
- **"How others see me vs how I see myself" is a proven needs-others web format:** the
  [Johari Window on kevan.org](https://kevan.org/johari) (you pick adjectives for yourself, send a
  link, friends pick for you, a grid shows overlap) has run for many years with public result pages
  ([FAQ](https://kevan.org/joharifaq.html)). It is text-only and individual, not a group chart.
  Newer install-only apps do the same: [TrueSelfIQ](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trueselfiq/id6756308966),
  [Blindspot](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6759895630), [Frienk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/frienk/id1672013990).
- **How people do it now:** one person makes the chart in TierMaker or a meme editor and posts it
  to the chat; everyone argues with it. The argument is the fun, but the chart is one person's
  opinion and nobody else's input is captured.
- **Gap:** no search-volume figure for "friend alignment chart"; demand evidence is the number of
  user-made templates and the meme's persistence, not volume.

## Our edge

- **Loop: needs others.** Your position is decided by the group; alone there is nothing.
- **K estimate ≈ 1.6** = 7 invited per chart (a typical friend chat) × 65% who place everyone
  (drag-only, under a minute, and being placed by friends is irresistible) × 35% of joiners who later
  chart another group. Reason: everyone is in several group chats and wants a second opinion of
  themselves; the "self vs friends" gap is personal enough to post. Weakest factor: joiner →
  creator; the reveal must end on "Chart your other group" with one tap.
- Beats TierMaker and meme editors on the thing that matters: the chart is the group's verdict,
  not one person's. Beats Johari-style apps on fun, speed and a visual result, and needs no install.
- **Risks:** meanness (a group pushing one person to "evil"). Mitigations: playful theme library by
  default (no "ugly/smart" axes), custom axes are word-filtered, averages hide individual votes,
  anyone can hide their own dot before the reveal, creator can delete the chart. 13+ copy.
