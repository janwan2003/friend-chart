Questions

What is a friend group alignment chart?

It's the meme where a friend group gets sorted onto a 3×3 grid (lawful good to chaotic evil) or onto two silly axes. Usually one person makes it and everyone argues. Here, the whole group votes, and each person's spot is the average of where their friends put them.

Can people see where I put them?

No. Before the reveal nobody sees anyone's answers. After the reveal you only see the group's average for each person and where each person put themselves, never who put whom where.

When does the chart reveal?

As soon as everyone in the group has placed everyone, or at the time limit the creator picked (3 hours, 1 day or 3 days), as long as at least 3 people have taken part. The creator can also reveal it early.

Do we need to sign up or install anything?

No. Open the link, type a nickname, done. It works in WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram and Messenger browsers, on any phone or computer.

Can I stay off the chart?

Yes. Anyone can tap "Leave me off the chart" before the reveal and still place their friends. The creator can delete the chart at any time, and every chart is deleted automatically after 60 days.

Is it free?

Yes, free, with no ads and no account.
