Chartmates

Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Chartmates is a free game for friend groups. It is built to need as little data as possible.

What we store

  • For each chart: the chart's theme or custom labels, the time limit, and the group size.
  • For each person who joins: the nickname they type, a randomly assigned emoji and colour, and where they placed each person (two numbers per person).
  • A random key kept in your browser's local storage, so the site remembers which nickname is yours. We store only a hashed version of it.

What others can see

Before the reveal, others only see who has joined and who has finished placing. After the reveal, anyone with the link sees each person's average position and where they put themselves. Nobody, including the chart's creator, ever sees who placed whom where.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers.
  • No ads, no cookies, no analytics trackers, no selling of data.
  • To stop abuse we count requests per network for up to an hour, using a one-way hash of the IP address. It is never stored with your chart.
  • We keep simple totals (number of charts created and people joined per day), with no personal data.

Deleting

Every chart is deleted automatically 60 days after it was created. The person who created a chart can delete it at any time from the chart page. Anyone can choose "Leave me off the chart" before the reveal.

Age

Chartmates is meant for people aged 13 and over. The built-in charts are playful by design, and custom charts with hurtful words are blocked.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare. Requests pass through Cloudflare's network, which processes IP addresses to deliver the site.

Contact

Questions or a request to remove a chart: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/friend-chart/issues.
